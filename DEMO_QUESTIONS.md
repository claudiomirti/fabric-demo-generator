# 🤖 Data Agent Demo Questions

These questions are designed to be asked against the semantic model / data agent
built on top of the datasets generated by this app.  Use them in:

- **Microsoft Fabric** — Copilot in the semantic model or Power BI report
- **Azure AI Agent** / **OpenAI Assistant** connected to the Lakehouse
- **Power BI Q&A** visual

Questions are grouped by industry and progress from simple lookups to
multi-table analytical questions.

---

## 🛒 Retail

### Sales Performance
- What is the total revenue for the last 30 days?
- Which product category generated the most revenue this year?
- Show me the top 10 best-selling products by quantity sold.
- What is the average order value per sales channel (In-Store vs Online vs Mobile)?
- Which store has the highest total sales this quarter?

### Customer Insights
- How many unique customers made a purchase last month?
- What is the revenue breakdown by customer loyalty tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)?
- Which city has the highest number of active customers?
- What percentage of revenue comes from Gold and Platinum loyalty customers?

### Discount & Margin
- What is the average discount rate applied across all transactions?
- Which product category has the highest average discount?
- Show total revenue lost to discounts per month.

### Multi-table / Trend
- Compare total revenue by store type (Flagship vs Outlet vs Express) over time.
- Which products are frequently bought online but rarely in-store?
- Show me the month-over-month revenue growth trend.

## 🏭 Manufacturing

### Production Efficiency
- What is the overall defect rate across all plants this month?
- Which machine has the highest average downtime?
- Show the top 5 products by total units produced.
- What is the average cycle time per product category?
- Which plant has the best OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)?

### Quality Control
- Which machines have a defect rate above 5%?
- How does defect rate vary by shift (Morning / Afternoon / Night)?
- Show the trend of defect counts over the last 6 months.

### Capacity & Downtime
- What is the total downtime in hours per plant this week?
- Which machine type (CNC, Press, Welder, etc.) experiences the most downtime?
- Compare units produced vs plant daily capacity — which plants are over 80% utilised?

### Multi-table / Trend
- Which products have an actual cycle time more than 20% above their target?
- Show production volume by shift and plant for the last quarter.
- Which machines are past their expected maintenance window based on manufacture year?

## 🏦 FSI (Financial Services)

### Transaction Analysis
- What is the total transaction volume processed this month?
- How many transactions were flagged as suspicious in the last 90 days?
- What is the average transaction value by channel (Branch, ATM, Online, Mobile, POS)?
- Which account type (Checking, Savings, Credit, Loan) has the highest total spend?

### Customer & Account Health
- How many customers have an expired KYC status?
- What is the average credit score across customer segments?
- Which branch has the most active accounts?
- How many accounts were opened in the last 12 months?

### Risk & Compliance
- What percentage of transactions are flagged as suspicious per channel?
- Show the trend of failed transactions over the last 6 months.
- Which customer segment has the highest proportion of flagged transactions?
- How many customers have both a low credit score (< 500) and a flagged transaction?

### Multi-table / Trend
- Compare transaction volumes across regions by month.
- Which branches have the highest value of pending transactions?
- Show the distribution of transaction amounts by account type.

## 🏥 Healthcare

### Patient & Encounter Overview
- How many patient encounters occurred in the last 30 days?
- What is the average length of stay across all facility types?
- Which facility has the highest total charges this year?
- How many encounters were Emergency vs Outpatient vs Telehealth?

### Readmission & Quality
- What is the 30-day readmission rate by facility?
- Which providers have the highest readmission rates?
- Show the average length of stay per diagnosis code.
- Which insurance type is associated with the highest average charge?

### Provider & Facility Performance
- Which specialty has the most encounters this quarter?
- Compare average charges between Hospital and Clinic facility types.
- Which facilities are running above 80% of their bed capacity?
- Show the top 5 providers by number of encounters.

### Multi-table / Trend
- How does the readmission rate trend over the last 12 months?
- Which diagnosis codes are most frequently associated with long stays (> 7 days)?
- Show revenue by encounter type and insurance type as a matrix.

## 🔬 Life Science

### Trial & Enrollment
- How many subjects are currently enrolled across all active trials?
- Which therapeutic area has the most trials in Phase III?
- Show the trial status breakdown (Recruiting, Active, Completed, Terminated).
- Which site has enrolled the most subjects?

### Safety & Adverse Events
- What is the overall adverse event rate across all trials?
- Which compound is associated with the highest rate of severe adverse events?
- Show the breakdown of adverse event types (Nausea, Fatigue, Headache, Dizziness).
- Which sites report the highest adverse event rates?

### Efficacy & Endpoints
- What is the average primary endpoint value per trial phase?
- Which compound shows the best primary endpoint response?
- Compare average endpoint values across therapeutic areas.

### Dropout & Retention
- What is the overall dropout rate across all trials?
- Which trial phase has the highest dropout rate?
- Do subjects with adverse events have a significantly higher dropout rate?

### Multi-table / Trend
- Show the primary endpoint trend over visit type (Baseline → Week 4 → Week 12 → Week 24).
- Which compounds are in both Phase II and Phase III trials?
- Compare adverse event rates by compound modality (Small Molecule vs Biologic vs Cell Therapy).
